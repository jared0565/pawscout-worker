# Active Paw affiliate terms - brand layer + assembly guide

> **Don't draft the agreement from scratch.** Assemble it: **UpPromote's built-in sample T&C** (the mechanics) **+ the brand layer below** (your policy + compliance, which the sample omits) **+ one UK-solicitor pass** (localise to UK law, make clawback/indemnity enforceable). This replaces the earlier full-bespoke draft - most of it was boilerplate UpPromote already provides.

Sources/refs: UpPromote [sample T&C](https://docs.uppromote.com/management/affiliate-registration-form/terms-conditions-sample) · `affiliate-program-terms-and-ruleset.md` (Parts B/E, the authority for these clauses) · `affiliate-media-gallery-brief.md` (the affiliate-facing version of the same rules).

---

## 1. What UpPromote's sample already covers ✅ vs what you must add ❌

**✅ In UpPromote's sample (use as-is, the base of the T&C field):** merchant approve/reject discretion · affiliate links + coupon assignment + last-click attribution · commission calculation on order value (ex-fees) and exclusion of chargebacks/refunds · cookie/attribution window (configurable) · marketing assets · MLM downline (leave OFF unless you want multi-level) · removal from the program.

**❌ NOT in the sample - add via the brand layer (§2):** ASA disclosure · **new-customer-only + eligible-SKU (£6 floor) + ex-VAT commission base** · validation/hold + clawback · prohibited activities (brand bidding, cookie-stuffing, self-referral, generic/stacked codes, coupon/cashback aggregators, rebating, unauthorised brand use) · **no UK-dispatch/origin claims (E.11)** · governing law/jurisdiction (the sample is country-neutral).

> Net: the sample is bare mechanics. Everything that protects margin, brand, and you legally is in §2. That's also why a US-template-and-go is risky - none of the UK compliance is in any generic template.

## 2. Brand layer - paste beneath UpPromote's sample in the Terms & Conditions field

*(Concise versions of ruleset Parts B + E. `[brackets]` = solicitor/accountant or program-config value.)*

**B1 - Commission eligibility.** Commission is **8% of net product value (excluding VAT, shipping, and refunds)**, paid on **new customers only** (matched against our customer list; returning-customer orders earn 0%), for **eligible SKUs only** (our published allowlist), attributed by your tracked link (primary) or your single locked code (secondary). Codes must not be stacked with other discounts.

**B2 - Validation & clawback.** Commission is held for a **validation period of at least 45 days** and is payable only once the order clears it. Refunded, cancelled or disputed orders earn nothing; clawback applies to **held/unvested commission only** (never sums already paid). *[solicitor: confirm vs UK penalty/unfair-terms law]*

**B3 - Disclosure (ASA).** You must label affiliate/sponsored content **#ad** (and **#ad gifted** for gifted items), **upfront**, before any "see more". *(We are jointly liable for an affiliate's undisclosed ad.)*

**B4 - Prohibited activities.** You must not: bid on or SEO-target our brand name "Active Paw" (incl. misspellings/variants/"+review/discount/code"), paid or organic; cookie-stuff, force clicks, or auto-redirect; self-refer (incl. fresh-email/guest-checkout variants); distribute generic codes or stack codes; post codes/links on coupon, cashback, voucher or browser-extension sites; rebate/kick back any commission to buyers; or make unauthorised use of our name, logo, or look-alike domains. Breach voids affected (held) commission and may end your participation.

**B5 - Honest delivery & origin (no UK-dispatch claims).** You must not state or imply that Active Paw ships, dispatches, holds stock, or is made **in the UK**, nor quote UK delivery speeds (e.g. "next-day", "2-4 day UK", "UK warehouse", "UK stock"). Products are fulfilled by our supplier from outside the UK. Approved phrasing: "ships internationally" / "delivered to your door". *(ruleset E.11)*

**B6 - Governing law.** This agreement is governed by **[England & Wales - confirm]** and subject to its courts.

## 3. Solicitor UK-localization checklist (the short list - this is the whole legal ask)

- [ ] **Disclosure → UK ASA/CMA** framing (the templates assume US FTC).
- [ ] **UK GDPR / PECR:** the affiliate tracking cookie is non-essential → requires consent; confirm wording + that rejection = broken attribution.
- [ ] **Clawback (B2)** enforceable under UK penalty / unfair-terms law (held/unvested only).
- [ ] **VAT split** by affiliate status (registered self-billing / unregistered plain statement / non-UK reverse charge) - **accountant**.
- [ ] **Liability cap + affiliate indemnity** for their content and any ASA/consumer-law breach.
- [ ] Fill the `[brackets]`: legal entity name + company no. + registered address, notice period, minimum payout threshold, governing law, cookie window (match UpPromote config).
