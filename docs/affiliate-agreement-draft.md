## 1. Definitions

- **Tracked Link / Code** — the unique affiliate link and the single, affiliate-locked discount code we issue you. *(ruleset B, E.5)*
- **New Customer** — a buyer with no prior purchase on the store, matched against our full customer list. *(ruleset B)*
- **Net Sales** — order product value **excluding VAT (20%), excluding shipping, and net of refunds/cancellations**. *(ruleset B#4)*
- **Eligible Products** — only SKUs on our published eligibility allowlist (which excludes items below our **£6 margin floor**). *(ruleset B; analysis §6)*
- **Qualifying Sale** — an order from a New Customer for Eligible Products, attributed to your Tracked Link or Code, that completes the Validation Period without refund, cancellation or dispute.
- **Validation Period** — **at least 45 days** from order before commission is payable. *(ruleset D#3, E.8)*

## 2. Appointment & admission

2.1 We appoint you as a non-exclusive affiliate. You are an independent contractor; this is not employment, agency, partnership or joint venture.
2.2 Admission is by **manual approval** and is discretionary. We may decline or later remove any affiliate. Eligibility targets **pet-relevant, UK-relevant content/review creators with a genuine, verified audience**; coupon/cashback/deal operators and unsolicited "guaranteed volume for a cut" pitches are not accepted. *(ruleset Part A)*
2.3 New affiliates may be placed on a **probationary reserve** before full payout terms apply. *(ruleset B)*

## 3. How you may promote

3.1 Promote only through your own approved channels with original content (reviews, how-to, UGC, honest mentions). Our intended differentiators are **exclusive bundles / value sets and your own audience and endorsement** — not delivery-speed or origin claims (see §5.9). *(analysis §3)*
3.2 Use only assets from our media gallery or your own honest content; follow the media-gallery brief.

## 4. Commission

4.1 **Rate: 8% of Net Sales** on Qualifying Sales. *(ruleset B)*
4.2 **New Customers only.** Returning-customer orders earn 0%. *(ruleset B)*
4.3 **Eligible Products only** (allowlist; sub-£6-floor SKUs excluded). *(ruleset B)*
4.4 Attribution is **Tracked-Link primary**, your **single locked Code secondary**; codes **must not be stacked** with other discounts. *(ruleset E.5)*
4.5 We may apply **per-order / rolling volume caps** and a probationary reserve. *(ruleset Part F)*
4.6 **Cookie / attribution window: [set to match program config — e.g. 14 days], last-click.** We may additionally report internally on first-click/multi-touch. *(ruleset B; analysis §4)*

## 5. Conduct & compliance *(ruleset Part E — binding)*

You agree that you will **not**, and that breach of any of the following voids affected commission (held/unvested only) and may lead to removal:

5.1 **Disclosure (ASA):** clearly label affiliate/sponsored posts **#ad** (and **#ad gifted** for gifted items), **upfront**, before any "see more". *(E.1 — we are jointly liable for an undisclosed ad)*
5.2 **No brand bidding or brand-term interception** on "Active Paw", misspellings, variants or "+review/discount/code" combinations — paid **or** organic/look-alike. *(E.2)*
5.3 **No cookie-stuffing, forced clicks, or auto-redirects.** *(E.3)*
5.4 **No self-referral** (including fresh-email/guest-checkout variants). *(E.4)*
5.5 **No generic code distribution;** your code is single-affiliate-locked and must not be stacked. *(E.5)*
5.6 **No posting codes/links on coupon/cashback aggregators or browser extensions.** *(E.6)*
5.7 **No customer rebating/kickback** (passing commission/cash back to buyers). *(E.9)*
5.8 **No unauthorised use** of our brand name, logo, or look-alike domains. *(E.10)*
5.9 **Honest delivery & origin (no UK-dispatch claims):** you must not state or imply that Active Paw ships, dispatches, holds stock, or is made **in the UK**, nor quote UK delivery speeds. Products are fulfilled by our supplier from outside the UK. Approved phrasing: "ships internationally" / "delivered to your door". *(E.11)*

## 6. Validation, hold & clawback

6.1 Commission is held for the **Validation Period (≥45 days)** and becomes payable only on a Qualifying Sale.
6.2 **Refunded, cancelled or disputed orders earn nothing.** Clawback reaches **held/unvested commission only** (not sums already paid), to stay within UK penalty/unfair-terms law. *(ruleset E.8 — **[solicitor: confirm clawback/penalty + unfair-terms compliance]**)*

7.1 **Method:** PayPal Payouts (primary) or store credit/gift card. **[Confirm minimum payout threshold + schedule.]**
7.2 **VAT (split by status):** VAT-registered affiliates → self-billing (20%); unregistered (under the £90k threshold) → plain commission statement, no VAT; non-UK B2B → reverse charge. **[Confirm with accountant.]** *(ruleset E.7)*
