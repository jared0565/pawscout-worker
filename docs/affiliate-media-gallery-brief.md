# Active Paw - Affiliate content brief ("what to say / what not to say")

Give this to every approved affiliate alongside their link and assets. It keeps content honest, on-brand, and compliant (ASA + our governing rules). The hard rules here mirror the Affiliate Agreement §5 and ruleset Part E - breaching them voids commission. Cross-ref: `affiliate-agreement-draft.md` §5, `affiliate-program-terms-and-ruleset.md` Part E, `affiliate-program-analysis.md` §3.

## Who we are (use this voice)
Active Paw is a pet-care brand for everyday dog & cat owners - curated picks for cleaning, accessories, health and toys. Warm, practical, no hype. Your **honest endorsement and your audience** are the value - not a hard sell.

## Your differentiator (what makes featuring us worth it)
Lead with **exclusive bundles / value sets** and your own genuine recommendation - *not* delivery speed or where we ship from. If a bundle or pick genuinely helps your audience, say why. (This is deliberately our angle - see "Do NOT" below for why delivery/origin claims are off-limits.)

## ✅ DO
- **Disclose every time:** put **#ad** (or **#ad gifted** for gifted items) **upfront**, before any "see more". This is legally required and protects us both.
- **Be honest about the product:** describe what's true from your own use/research; if you're unsure about a detail, leave it out or hedge.
- **Use your own Tracked Link / locked code** so sales attribute to you.
- **Talk delivery honestly** using only: **"ships internationally" / "delivered to your door"** - and, if you mention timing at all, set relaxed expectations ("allow a little time for delivery").
- **Tag us** (@activepaw) so we can reshare.
- **Ask us** if you're unsure whether a claim is OK - we'd rather you check.

## ⛔ Do NOT (these void commission)
- ❌ **No UK-dispatch / origin / speed claims.** Never say or imply we ship, dispatch, hold stock, or are made **in the UK**, and never quote UK delivery speeds (e.g. "next-day", "2-4 day UK", "UK warehouse", "UK stock"). Our products are fulfilled by our supplier from outside the UK - claiming otherwise is false and we're jointly liable. *(ruleset E.11)*
- ❌ **No brand bidding** - don't run ads on, or SEO-target, "Active Paw" (or misspellings/variants/"+review/discount/code"). *(E.2)*
- ❌ **No coupon/cashback/deal sites or browser extensions** - don't post your code or links there. *(E.6)*
- ❌ **No rebating** - don't offer to send buyers cash/commission back for using your code. *(E.9)*
- ❌ **No fake reviews, cookie-stuffing, forced clicks, or self-purchases.** *(E.3, E.4)*
- ❌ **No unverifiable health/medical claims** about pet products - stick to what's defensible (e.g. "my dog loved it", not "cures X").
- ❌ **No implying a relationship beyond affiliate** (you're not staff/spokesperson) and **no unauthorised use** of our logo/brand/look-alike domains. *(E.10)*

## Approved assets
Pull product images and approved copy from the **UpPromote media gallery**; or use your own original content. Don't alter the logo or invent claims not supported by the product page.

## If in doubt
Ask before you post. A quick check is always fine - an undisclosed ad or a false UK-shipping claim is not, and would cost you the commission on that content.
