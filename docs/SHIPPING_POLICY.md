Shipping Policy

Here's everything about how your Active Paw order reaches you.

DELIVERY CHARGES

- United Kingdom: standard delivery £4.99 - FREE on orders over £50.
- European Union: £14.99.
- Rest of world: £23.99.
- All charges are shown clearly at checkout before you pay.

HOW LONG IT TAKES

- Every product page shows an estimated delivery window for that item.
- Most items: 8–12 working days (processing within 1–3 working days, then tracked transit).
- Items marked "Ships from UK": 2–4 working days.
- Estimates are made in good faith but aren't guaranteed - carriers and customs can occasionally add delays. If your order is running late, contact us and we'll chase it and keep you updated.

TRACKING

You'll receive a dispatch email with a tracking link as soon as your order ships. You can also check your order status any time via the link in your confirmation email.

ORDERS WITH SEVERAL ITEMS

To get everything to you as fast as possible, items are dispatched from the nearest warehouse that stocks them - so a multi-item order may arrive as more than one parcel, sometimes days apart. You'll only ever pay one delivery charge per order. If part of your order has arrived and you're waiting on the rest, your tracking links show each parcel separately.

DELIVERY ADDRESSES

- Please double-check your delivery address at checkout - it's confirmed in your order email, and you can correct any mistake free of charge by contacting us before dispatch.
- We can't redirect a parcel once it has shipped, and we can't be responsible for parcels delivered to an incorrectly entered address. If that happens, get in touch and we'll do our best to help.
- We currently deliver to the UK, EU and the countries shown at checkout. We can't deliver to PO boxes or freight forwarders.

CUSTOMS AND IMPORT CHARGES (EU AND INTERNATIONAL ORDERS)

Orders delivered outside the UK may be subject to import duties, taxes or handling fees charged by the destination country. These are set by your local authorities, are not included in our prices or delivery charges, and are the recipient's responsibility. UK orders never have customs charges.

LOST OR UNDELIVERED PARCELS

If tracking shows no movement for several days, or your parcel is marked delivered but hasn't arrived, contact us at contact@activepaw.co.uk. We'll investigate with the carrier and, where a parcel is confirmed lost, send a replacement or a full refund - your choice.

QUESTIONS

contact@activepaw.co.uk - we reply within one working day.
