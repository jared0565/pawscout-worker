What the platform is

PawScout is a commerce automation platform that runs the operational side of a supplier-backed Shopify store: it answers customer email with grounded AI agents, keeps the catalog priced and stocked from live supplier data, computes honest delivery promises, and gives the owner a consent-safe marketing suite. Active Paw is its companion storefront: a fast, installable, conversion-focused Shopify theme wired to the same live data.

The platform's governing rule is that customers are only ever shown information that is real and verifiable at its source. Stock levels, delivery estimates and shipping origins come from live supplier and logistics data, never from guesses, and the AI reply system is built so that an unknown produces a safe hedge rather than an invented answer.

1. AI customer inbox

Inbound customer email is ingested, classified and answered by specialised agents, with the owner in control of exactly how much automation to allow.

FeatureWhat it does
Email ingestion & threadingInbound mail on the store's own domain is captured, parsed (including attachments and forwarded chains) and grouped into conversation threads.
AI classificationEvery message is sorted into a category: product questions, order status, shipping questions, complaints, wholesale enquiries, partnership pitches, affiliate applications, and general mail.
Specialised reply agentsEach category has its own purpose-built agent. Shipping replies quote real transit bands from the synced logistics matrix; order-status replies read the actual order; product replies are grounded in the live catalog.
Human-in-the-loop by defaultAgents produce drafts for one-click owner approval. Auto-send can be enabled per agent, so trusted categories graduate to full automation one at a time.
Draft quality loopEvery outbound draft passes an automated review-and-refine cycle inside the platform before it is offered or sent, plus deterministic output rules (voice, formatting, claim discipline).
Fabrication gateReplies about the business itself are checked against a grounded business profile so the AI cannot invent history, addresses or capabilities.
Invoicing from a threadWholesale and custom requests can be turned into a Shopify draft order and priced invoice email directly from the conversation, with a price floor guard.
Attribution & alertingConversations link to the orders they produce; urgent categories can alert the owner by email immediately.

2. Catalog and supplier intelligence

The catalog is treated as a live contract with the supplier (CJDropshipping), not a one-time import.

FeatureWhat it does
Product discovery & importSupplier products are discovered by category, screened, and imported with AI-curated titles, descriptions and hero image selection. Curated add-on (basket-filler) products import alongside.
Daily stock syncSupplier stock is written into Shopify tracked inventory every night; products that fall under a stock floor are automatically zeroed and paused, and recover automatically when restocked.
Pricing engineRetail prices follow configurable rules from live supplier cost (freight included), with margin caps and floors, so the sticker price is always honest and profitable.
Supplier metafields contractAvailability, quantity, dispatch origin, delivery estimate and freshness stamps are written to a documented set of Shopify metafields the storefront reads. The theme never calls supplier APIs directly.
Quantity capsProduct page and cart enforce a per-line quantity cap from tracked inventory or supplier limits, so customers cannot order more than can be fulfilled.
Real-demand badges"Selling fast" style flags are set only from genuine recent order events, never invented.
Semantic search libraryProduct knowledge is indexed so search and agents can match customer language to catalog items.

3. Shipping and delivery intelligence

One formula governs every delivery claim on every surface: the nearest warehouse to the customer's destination is the origin, and times come from the supplier's live logistics matrix.

FeatureWhat it does
Logistics matrix syncPer-route transit times and freight costs are synced from the supplier and freshness-gated: stale data degrades to a safe hedge instead of a wrong number.
Per-product delivery estimatesEvery product carries an order-to-door day range (for example "3-10 days" for locally stocked items) computed from processing plus door transit plus a small safety margin, refreshed nightly and shown on the product page and at checkout.
Nearest-warehouse resolverShipping quotes in customer replies and checkout profiles both use the same resolver, so the quote a customer receives matches what checkout charges.
Shipping profiles syncShopify delivery profiles are built automatically from each product's warehouse signature, with local items shipping free domestically where supplier freight is genuinely zero.
Market availability controlProducts that cannot be fulfilled to a market are excluded from that market's catalog at checkout level, so an unfulfillable order cannot be placed.
Honest-origin policyThe storefront gives calculated delivery times without claiming a dispatch origin; local-stock claims appear only for source-verified locally warehoused items.

4. Marketing and growth suite

FeatureWhat it does
CampaignsOwner-composed broadcasts to consented audiences. Buyer consent is read live from Shopify at send time; partners require a recorded opt-in. Batch sending with a per-recipient ledger means nobody is ever emailed twice.
Campaign lifecycle controlsDrafts are editable and testable; a live send can be paused, resumed or stopped; finished campaigns duplicate into fresh drafts. Consequential actions need a confirming second click.
Email engagement analyticsPer-campaign delivered, opened and clicked counts (unique recipients). Hard bounces and spam complaints are auto-suppressed to protect sender reputation.
Compliance footerEvery marketing email carries an identified sender, the business postal address, and a working one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058). Sending is blocked until a postal address is saved.
Partner / affiliate programApplications arrive through the inbox, invitations issue tracked voucher codes, orders attribute to the code, and every partner's performance is visible. Vouchers can be revoked; revocation deactivates the code.
Welcome offer machineryA storefront welcome event (discount code modal with real end date) with automated voucher issue and trial-entry gating.
Reorder remindersConsent-gated retention email for consumable repurchases, held dormant until order volume justifies it.
Traffic strategy boardA live acquisition-channel board (status, priority, next action per channel) seeded with a store-specific starter strategy, documented in the built-in knowledge base.

6. Compliance and trust, by construction

FeatureWhat it does
Consent enforcement in codeMarketing audiences are resolved from live consent state at the moment of sending. A shared suppression list covers every marketing surface; order email is never affected.
Privacy policy keeperThe console checks the live privacy policy against recommended wording (opt-in marketing, engagement measurement, unsubscribe, business address) and applies missing pieces in one owner click.
Claim disciplineDeterministic gates keep customer-facing text free of unverifiable claims: no invented fulfilment locations, no fabricated business history, conservative fallbacks when data is missing.
Output hygieneAll outbound text passes runtime sanitisation (typography rules, formatting discipline) so automated mail reads like a person wrote it.
Audit trailAn owner-facing audit view records what the automation did and why, with health stamps for every scheduled job.

8. Platform and security

FeatureWhat it does
Multi-tenant coreEvery table, credential and configuration is scoped per shop. One deployment serves many stores with strict data isolation.
Encrypted credential vaultSupplier keys, tokens and webhook secrets are sealed with a per-tenant data key wrapped by a platform master key. Secrets never appear in logs or leave the worker.
Managed Shopify installStandard Shopify app install with token exchange; no manual API keys, scope upgrades handled through app releases.
Edge infrastructureRuns on Cloudflare Workers with a D1 database: globally fast, no servers for the tenant to run, scheduled jobs built in.
IntegrationsShopify Admin GraphQL, CJDropshipping API, Resend transactional email (with signed event webhooks), and a bring-your-own-key AI model layer with a curated model catalog.
TestedThe platform ships with an extensive automated test suite covering the sending machinery, consent gates, pricing, logistics maths and the console itself.
